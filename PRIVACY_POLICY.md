# Circuit60 Privacy Policy

Circuit60 stores workout records, benchmark results, measurements, photographs, settings, and optional HealthKit export state only on your device. The app does not operate a server and does not send your content to the developer.

## What Circuit60 does not do

- No accounts.
- No advertising.
- No analytics.
- No tracking.
- No data sale.
- No third-party runtime dependencies.

## HealthKit

HealthKit integration is optional and off by default. When enabled, Circuit60 can save completed workout summaries to Health. You can disable it at any time in Settings. Disabling the integration does not delete data already stored by the Health app.

## Local backups

When you create a backup, the app writes a file that you choose to share or store. You control that file after export. Restoring a backup replaces local Circuit60 data after confirmation.

## Children

Circuit60 is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
