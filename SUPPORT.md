# Circuit60 Support

## Version

Current app version: 1.0.0.

## Before you reinstall

1. Open Circuit60.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Tap **Export backup**.
4. Save the `.c60archive` file outside the app.

## If a workout stops

1. Reopen Circuit60.
2. Go to the Train tab.
3. Resume the recovered session.

Your timer keeps its own elapsed time. It does not count paused time.

## Health and safety

Circuit60 gives general fitness information. It does not give medical advice.

Stop training and get medical help if you feel chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness, faintness, or palpitations.

## Report a problem

Use the public support repository:

https://github.com/GDemay/circuit60-support/issues

Include:

- App version.
- iPhone model.
- iOS version.
- What you expected.
- What happened.
- A screenshot, if it helps.

Do not attach private progress photos.
